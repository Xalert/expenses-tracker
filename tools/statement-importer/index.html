Statement Importer

Turns bank and card statements into the tracker's transactions CSV. Claude reads each statement; you say which wallet and category things belong to, once, and the page remembers.

  1. Your wallets and categories

    Load them once from your tracker (/data → Wallets & categories: Download or Copy), or type them in. Only names, currencies and kinds are kept.

  2. Drop statements

    CSV, text, PDF or a photo of a statement, several at once. Claude finds the transactions; you say which wallet each statement is.

  3. Review and download

    Check categories and transfers between your own accounts, then import the CSV at /data in your tracker.

Wallets & categories

Load them from your tracker

In the tracker, open /data → Wallets & categories (JSON) and press Download, then choose the file here or drop it anywhere on this panel, or press Copy and paste it below. That file has only wallet names and currencies and category names and kinds, no transactions; a full backup file also works, but only its wallets and categories are read.

The file and pasted text are not kept; archived wallets are left out.
